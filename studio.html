News studio

Write a post for the news page. You do not need to know any code, install anything, or open any other file. Fill this in, check the preview, press the two download buttons, and send what you get to whoever looks after the site.

  1. Step 1Write the post in the form. The preview updates as you type.
  2. Step 2Drop in a photo if you have one. It is shrunk for the web automatically.
  3. Step 3Press Add this post, then the two download buttons.
  4. Step 4Send both downloaded files on. That is the whole job.
